Visa Services

Do you guarantee visas?

No. Visa approval is solely at the discretion of the respective embassy or immigration authority. However, we significantly improve your chances by ensuring your application is complete, accurate, professionally prepared, and compliant with the embassy's requirements.

How long does visa processing take?

Processing times vary depending on the country, visa category, and embassy workload. Some visas may take a few days, while others can take several weeks or months. We will advise you on the estimated processing timeline before submission.

What documents are required?

Required documents vary depending on the visa type and destination country. Common requirements include: International Passport, Passport Photographs, Bank Statements, Employment or Business Documents, Invitation Letter (where applicable), Flight Itinerary, Hotel Reservation, Travel Insurance, and Supporting Financial Documents. Our consultants will provide you with a personalized checklist based on your application.

Do you ship internationally and how long does delivery take?

Yes. We provide reliable international and domestic logistics solutions. Delivery times depend on the destination, shipping method, customs clearance, and carrier schedules. We will provide an estimated delivery timeframe once your shipment is booked. Shipment tracking is available for eligible courier and freight services.

What is document legalization?

Document legalization is the official process of verifying and certifying documents so they are legally recognized by foreign governments, universities, employers, or institutions. This may involve notarization, authentication by government authorities, and embassy legalization.
